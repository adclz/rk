---
title: Address not locatable
---
A variable of a FUNCTION, FUNCTION_BLOCK or CLASS takes no complete address. In a block, declare it `AT %I*` and locate each instance in `VAR_CONFIG`. Elsewhere, declare it in a PROGRAM or a VAR_GLOBAL.

A STRUCT field takes no address: it is part of every variable of its type. Declare the channel in a FUNCTION_BLOCK instead, where `VAR_CONFIG` locates it per instance.

A partial address like `%I*` goes on a variable of a PROGRAM, FUNCTION_BLOCK or CLASS, and names its area alone.

An address names its area with `I`, `Q` or `M` and its width with `X`, `B`, `W`, `D` or `L`. A library names no complete address.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Valve
VAR
    POS AT %QW28 : INT;
END_VAR
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
