---
title: Address cannot be located
---
A variable of a FUNCTION, FUNCTION_BLOCK or CLASS cannot have a complete address: in a block, declare it `AT %I*` and locate each instance in `VAR_CONFIG`, else declare it in a PROGRAM or a VAR_GLOBAL.

A partial address like `%I*` only goes on a variable of a PROGRAM, FUNCTION_BLOCK or CLASS, and names its area alone. The area is `I`, `Q` or `M`, the width `X`, `B`, `W`, `D` or `L`, and a library names no complete address.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Valve
VAR
    POS AT %QW28 : INT;
END_VAR
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
