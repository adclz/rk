---
title: Address cannot be located
---
A directly represented variable (`%IX0.0`, `%QW4`) is storage in one of three bands: `%I`, `%Q` or `%M`. Written bare in a body, or bound with `AT` to a `VAR_GLOBAL` or to a PROGRAM's `VAR`, it is stored there, as part of a wider address when the program also names one.

Three kinds of address cannot be:

- An `AT` clause with a complete address on a variable of a function, function block or class. Its variables belong to each call or instance, so one address cannot be theirs. In a function block or class, declare it `AT %I*`, `%Q*` or `%M*` and give each instance its address in `VAR_CONFIG`. Elsewhere, declare it in a PROGRAM, or as a `VAR_GLOBAL` of the CONFIGURATION, and name it from there.
- A partly specified address, `%I*`, anywhere but a variable of a PROGRAM, function block or class. `VAR_CONFIG` completes it for each instance of those; a `VAR_GLOBAL` or a function's variable has no instances, so write its address in full. A partly specified address names its area and nothing else: `%IW*` and `%Z*` are refused everywhere.
- An address whose area or width letter is not one of `I`, `Q`, `M` and `X`, `B`, `W`, `D`, `L`, such as `%Z0` or `%IZ0`. A bit may leave its width out: `%I1` is `%IX1`.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Valve
VAR
    POS AT %QW28 : INT;
END_VAR
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
