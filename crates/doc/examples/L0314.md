---
title: Boolean operators mixed without parentheses
lint_rule: mixed-boolean-operators
---
AND, XOR and OR are mixed in one expression without parentheses.

AND is applied first, then XOR, then OR: `a AND b OR c AND d` means `(a AND b) OR (c AND d)`.
Writing those parentheses makes the order plain to anyone reading the line.

```iecst
FUNCTION Run : BOOL
VAR_INPUT
    auto, ready, manual, jog : BOOL;
END_VAR
    Run := auto AND ready OR manual AND jog;
END_FUNCTION
```
