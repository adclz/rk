---
title: Boolean operators mixed without parentheses
lint_rule: mixed-boolean-operators
---
AND, XOR and OR are mixed in one expression, without the parentheses that say which goes first.

AND binds tighter than XOR, and XOR tighter than OR: `a AND b OR c AND d` is `(a AND b) OR (c AND d)`.
Writing the parentheses shows that order to a reader who may have read it another way.

```iecst
FUNCTION Run : BOOL
VAR_INPUT
    auto, ready, manual, jog : BOOL;
END_VAR
    Run := auto AND ready OR manual AND jog;
END_FUNCTION
```
