---
title: Storage larger than 4 GiB
---
A module's sizes and addresses are 32 bits, so nothing it stores takes more than 4 GiB. 

An array, a STRING, a STRUCT or an instance past that is refused where its size first passes it: a variable of a TYPE too large is reported at the TYPE.

```iecst
TYPE Samples : ARRAY[0..999999999] OF LREAL; END_TYPE
```
