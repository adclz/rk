---
title: Ambiguous namespace
---
A library's namespaces can be named without the library's name before them, `USING Motion` or `Motion.Axis`, while one library alone declares the namespace. When two libraries declare it, the name says nothing: write the library's name before it, `Drives.Motion` or `Util.Motion`.

A library's name is the `name` of its `config.toml`, and the namespace everything it declares is in.
