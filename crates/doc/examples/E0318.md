---
title: Assignment to an instance
---
A FUNCTION_BLOCK or CLASS instance cannot be assigned.

Pass the instance as a VAR_IN_OUT, or assign its members one by one.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK fb1
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK

FUNCTION_BLOCK fb2
VAR
    inst : fb1;
END_VAR
    inst := 0;
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
