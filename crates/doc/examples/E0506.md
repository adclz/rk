---
title: Constant index out of bounds
---
A constant subscript outside the array's declared bounds is provable at compile time and rejected here, instead of faulting the scan at runtime.
Checked per dimension.

```iecst
FUNCTION f1 : INT
VAR
    a : ARRAY[0..2] OF INT;
END_VAR
    a[5] := 1;
END_FUNCTION
```
