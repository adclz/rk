---
title: Partial access out of range
---
A partial access names a part of its base: bit 0 to 15 of a WORD with `%X`, byte 0 or 1 with `%B`.

An offset past the last part, or a part wider than the base, is refused.

```iecst
FUNCTION fn1 : INT
VAR
    w : WORD;
    b : BOOL;
END_VAR
    b := w.%X16;
    fn1 := 1;
END_FUNCTION
```
