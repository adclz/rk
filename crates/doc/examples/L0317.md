---
title: VAR that could be a VAR_TEMP
lint_rule: var-could-be-temp
---
A VAR of a FUNCTION_BLOCK or PROGRAM is written before it is read, each time the body runs.

A VAR keeps its value from one run to the next.
This one never needs to.
Declared in a `VAR_TEMP` section, it tells the reader so.
It also stops taking room in every instance.
The counter of a FOR loop is the usual case.

A variable used by a method, read from outside the block, handed out by `REF()`, `RETAIN`, `CONSTANT` or located is not reported.
The rule cannot see a debugger or an HMI reading a variable between runs.
Allow the rule on such a variable.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Average
VAR_INPUT
    values : ARRAY[1..10] OF REAL;
END_VAR
VAR_OUTPUT
    mean : REAL;
END_VAR
VAR
    i : INT;
    sum : REAL;
END_VAR
    sum := 0.0;
    FOR i := 1 TO 10 DO
        sum := sum + values[i];
    END_FOR;
    mean := sum / 10.0;
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
