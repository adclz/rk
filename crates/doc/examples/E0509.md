---
title: ARRAY[*] not allowed here
---
An `ARRAY[*]` takes its bounds from each call. It is a VAR_INPUT, VAR_OUTPUT or VAR_IN_OUT of a FUNCTION or a METHOD, or a VAR_IN_OUT of a FUNCTION_BLOCK.

An `ARRAY[*]` with no `OF`, of any type, is a VAR_INPUT or a VAR_IN_OUT of a FUNCTION.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Totalizer
VAR_INPUT
    values : ARRAY[*] OF INT;
END_VAR
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
