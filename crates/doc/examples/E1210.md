---
title: jump out of a __FINALLY
---
A `__FINALLY` runs on the way out of its `__TRY`, and also while an exception leaves it.

A `RETURN`, an `EXIT` or a `CONTINUE` out of it would end that exception with no `__CATCH`, and the program would go on as if nothing had been raised.
Write the jump after `__END_TRY`.

A `__RAISE` in a `__FINALLY` is accepted: it puts another exception in the place of the one leaving.

```iecst
FUNCTION f1 : INT
    __TRY
        f1 := 1;
    __FINALLY
        RETURN;
    __END_TRY;
END_FUNCTION
```
