---
title: jump out of a __FINALLY
---
A `__FINALLY` runs on the way out of its `__TRY`, with or without an exception pending.

A `RETURN`, an `EXIT` or a `CONTINUE` that leaves it would drop the exception.
Write the jump after `__END_TRY`.

```iecst
FUNCTION f1 : INT
    __TRY
        f1 := 1;
    __FINALLY
        RETURN;
    __END_TRY;
END_FUNCTION
```
