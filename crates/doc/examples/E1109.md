---
title: SUPER() called in a method
---
`SUPER()` runs the body of the base FUNCTION_BLOCK, and only the FUNCTION_BLOCK body calls it. A method reaches the base's methods with `SUPER.Method()`.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK base
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK

FUNCTION_BLOCK derived EXTENDS base
    METHOD m1
        SUPER()
    END_METHOD
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
