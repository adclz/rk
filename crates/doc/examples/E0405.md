---
title: Initializer for a member that takes none
---
An instance initializer, `d : Drive := (k := 4)`, sets the members that hold a value of their own.

A VAR_IN_OUT is given in the call, a VAR_TEMP or a CONSTANT in its declaration, a VAR_EXTERNAL on its global.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Drive
VAR_IN_OUT io : INT; END_VAR
VAR k : INT; END_VAR
    k := io;
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK

PROGRAM P
VAR d : Drive := (io := 30); END_VAR
END_PROGRAM
```
