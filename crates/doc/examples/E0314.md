---
title: STRING literal over its capacity
---
The literal is longer than the `STRING[n]` it fills: a variable, a field's default, an element of an initializer, an input argument, the result or a store through `^`.

Widen the declaration or shorten the literal. Nothing is truncated silently.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK fb1
VAR
    s: STRING[2] := 'hello';
END_VAR
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
