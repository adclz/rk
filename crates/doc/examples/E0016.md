---
title: SUPER after a dot in a path
---
`SUPER` must be the first element of a path expression, as in `SUPER.m()`.

It cannot appear as a member after a dot — `THIS.SUPER.m()` and `inst.SUPER.x` are rejected.

(Using a correctly placed `SUPER` in a POU with no EXTENDS clause is E1107; using it in a POU that cannot extend at all, such as a FUNCTION, is E1106.)

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK base
METHOD m
END_METHOD
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK

FUNCTION_BLOCK derived EXTENDS base
    THIS.SUPER.m();
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
