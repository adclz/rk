---
title: Initial value on shared bytes
---
When `config.toml` numbers addresses by byte, with `numbering = "byte"` under `[addresses]`, two addresses can share bytes with neither holding the other: `%MW7` and `%MW8` share byte 8. An initial value of one would set part of the other, so neither can have one, whether its declaration or a `VAR_CONFIG` entry gives it.

To start them from a value, locate a variable at an address that holds all their bytes, like `%MD7`, and give it the initial value: the others are then parts of it.
