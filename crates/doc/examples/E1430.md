---
title: Frame larger than the stack
---
A POU that calls itself, directly or through others, pushes a frame on the stack at each call, for its arrays, strings, structures and instances. `stack_size` in `config.toml` sets the size of the stack, and a frame larger than it could never be pushed.

The frame is reported at the POU, or at the call that creates a copy of it for the arrays it binds to an `ARRAY[*]` parameter. Without `stack_size`, the stack holds the largest frame and 64 KiB more.
