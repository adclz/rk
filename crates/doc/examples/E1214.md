---
title: FOR bound of another type
---
A FOR loop reads its final value and its step at the type of its counter, so each is of that type or widens to it.

A wider one takes a conversion, or a wider counter.

```iecst
FUNCTION f : INT
VAR i : INT; last : DINT; END_VAR
    FOR i := 1 TO last DO
        f := i;
    END_FOR;
END_FUNCTION
```
