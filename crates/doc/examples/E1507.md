---
title: Wasm operands of the wrong type
---
The operands of a `{wasm}` pragma do not match the instruction's signature.

The message shows what the instruction takes and what it is given.

```iecst
FUNCTION Nearest : DINT
VAR_INPUT
    IN : DINT;
END_VAR
    {wasm 'f32.nearest' (params IN) (result Nearest)}
END_FUNCTION
```
