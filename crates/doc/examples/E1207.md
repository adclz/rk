---
title: CASE selector of the wrong type
---
`CASE` branches on an integer, a bit string, a CHAR, an enum or a STRING. Branch with `IF` on a REAL, a duration or a date. A BOOL is refused too: it has two values, and `IF` tells them apart.

```iecst
FUNCTION Pick : INT
VAR_INPUT r : REAL; END_VAR
    CASE r OF
        1: Pick := 1;
    END_CASE;
END_FUNCTION
```
