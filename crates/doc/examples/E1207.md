---
title: CASE selector of the wrong type
---
`CASE` branches on an integer, a bit string, a CHAR, an enum or a STRING.
A literal selector takes its type from its labels, or its default one when they are literals too: `CASE 1.5 OF` is a REAL.

A REAL, a duration or a date is branched on with `IF`. So is a BOOL.

```iecst
FUNCTION Pick : INT
VAR_INPUT r : REAL; END_VAR
    CASE r OF
        1: Pick := 1;
    END_CASE;
END_FUNCTION
```
