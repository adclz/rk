---
title: Variable and method with the same name
lint_rule: variable-method-name
---
A variable and a method of a block, or of one it extends, have the same name. This is legal, as in CODESYS and TwinCAT, but easy to misread.

Inside the block the name is the variable, `THIS` included. From outside an instance, a variable the block keeps to itself gives way to the method.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Outer
VAR
    step : INT;
END_VAR
    METHOD STEP END_METHOD
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
