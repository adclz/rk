---
title: Partial access to a type without parts
---
A partial access reads or writes a bit, a byte, a word or a double word of a bit string, an integer or a BOOL.

The bits of a REAL encode the number rather than holding parts of it. To work on them, convert the REAL with `REAL_TO_DWORD` and access the parts of the DWORD.

```iecst
FUNCTION fn1 : INT
VAR
    r : REAL;
    b : BOOL;
END_VAR
    b := r.31;
    fn1 := 1;
END_FUNCTION
```
