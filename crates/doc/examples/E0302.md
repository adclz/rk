---
title: Types not comparable
---
The two sides of a comparison have types that do not convert to each other: an INT and a STRING, a TIME and an INT.

A type with no comparison at all, such as a STRUCT under `=`, is E0305.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK fb1
VAR
    x : INT;
    y : STRING;
    z : BOOL;
END_VAR
    z := x > y;
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
