---
title: Method declaration in body
---
A METHOD declaration cannot appear inside the body (statement list) of a POU.
Methods must be declared at the top level of a FUNCTION_BLOCK or CLASS.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK fb1
VAR
    x: INT;
END_VAR
    x := 1;
    METHOD m1
    END_METHOD
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
