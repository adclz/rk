---
title: SUPER() called inside a loop
---
The call of `SUPER()` shall not be in a loop (`FOR`/`WHILE`/`REPEAT`).

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK base
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK

FUNCTION_BLOCK derived EXTENDS base
    VAR i : INT; END_VAR
    FOR i := 1 TO 3 DO
        SUPER();
    END_FOR
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK
```
