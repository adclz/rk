---
title: Part of a wider address
---
An address inside a wider one the program also names is part of it, with no storage of its own: beside a `%QW0`, `%QX0.3` is its bit 3.

A bit cannot be passed to a VAR_IN_OUT of a `FUNCTION` or a `METHOD`, which binds its address, or referenced, and no part can be RETAIN or NON_RETAIN, or have an initial value. A `FUNCTION_BLOCK` copies a scalar VAR_IN_OUT in and back, and takes a bit.

```iecst
FUNCTION Flip : BOOL
VAR_IN_OUT v : BOOL; END_VAR
    v := NOT v;
    Flip := v;
END_FUNCTION

PROGRAM P
VAR w : WORD; b : BOOL; END_VAR
    w := %QW0;
    b := Flip(v := %QX0.3);
END_PROGRAM
```
