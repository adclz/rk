---
title: Part of a wider address
---
An address inside a wider one the program also names is part of it, with no storage of its own: beside a `%QW0`, `%QX0.3` is its bit 3.

A bit cannot be passed to a VAR_IN_OUT or referenced, and no part can be RETAIN or have an initial value.

```iecst
FUNCTION_BLOCK Flip
VAR_IN_OUT v : BOOL; END_VAR
    v := NOT v;
END_FUNCTION_BLOCK

PROGRAM P
VAR f : Flip; w : WORD; END_VAR
    w := %QW0;
    f(v := %QX0.3);
END_PROGRAM
```
