---
title: RETAIN mismatch on shared bytes
---
When `config.toml` numbers addresses by byte, with `numbering = "byte"` under `[addresses]`, two addresses can share bytes with neither holding the other: `%MW7` is bytes 7 and 8, and `%MW8` bytes 8 and 9. They are stored together, and persist together or not at all: when the variable located at one of them is `RETAIN`, the variables located at the others must be `RETAIN` too.

An address inside one of them is part of it, and its persistence is that address's to declare (`E1423`).
